# About

I’m Jason Nuttle, an artist and engineer based in New Haven, Connecticut.

I make things that move, make sound, and invite people to play. My work moves between kinetic sculpture, musical instruments, and experiments with mechanisms and electronics. I’m interested in how a simple motion or an unexpected sound can give an object a sense of life.

I manage the studio at Yale’s Center for Engineering Innovation & Design, helping people turn ideas into physical things. I graduated from Yale in 2025 with degrees in Art and Engineering Sciences.

You can explore my [projects](/projects/) or find more experiments and work in progress on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/nuttlejason/).
