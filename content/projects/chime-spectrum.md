**Medium:** Dichroic Plastic, Aluminum, Steel, Amplification and Other Custom Electronics

Chime Spectrum is a multi-sensory, musical installation designed to be placed in an outdoor space. Wind chimes and hammer rung chimes allow an approachable way to create music in collaboration with nature. Dichroic disks cover the surrounding space with a dazzling array of colors. The bass chimes are amplified through two custom speakers.

Created in partnership with [Creative Machines](https://www.creativemachines.com/).
