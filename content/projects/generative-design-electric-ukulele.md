**Medium:** PLA, Wood

This ukulele was designed using Fusion 360's generative design features. The body was 3D printed in two parts and reinforced with an internal threaded rod. There is an under-saddle pickup for connection to an amp.

Currently partnering with Evolve Additive Solutions to develop a metal plated version.
