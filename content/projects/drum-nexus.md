**Medium:** Armorite MDF, Stainless Steel, Buckets, Triangle, Custom Electronics.

Drum Nexus is a collaborative space for exploring rhythms. The drum machine allows for swinging, polyrhythms, and other techniques usually unavailable on drum machines.

Created in partnership with [Creative Machines](https://www.creativemachines.com/).
