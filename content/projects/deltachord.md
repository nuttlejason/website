**Medium:** Teensy 4.1, Acrylic, Old Cigar Box, Various Electronics

A teensy-based synth designed after the classic Suzuki Omnichord. It is a sort of electronic autoharp, allowing anyone to make very beautiful sounds in very little time.

A custom chord generating algorithm was developed for this project.
